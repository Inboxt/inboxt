# Reading in Inboxt

Inboxt is designed for reading. Articles and newsletters open in a clean reader view that removes distractions and keeps the focus on the content.

[[toc]]

## Reader view

When you open an article or newsletter, Inboxt displays it in a dedicated **reader view**, similar to reader modes in modern browsers.

The reader view provides a focused environment for your content with several key features:

- **Clean Layout**: Centers content and removes ads, navigation, and other visual noise.
- **Consistency**: Works seamlessly for both articles and newsletters.
- **Swipe Navigation**: On touch devices, you can swipe left or right to quickly navigate between articles.
- **Image Lightbox**: Click on any image within an article to view it in full screen.
- **Link Actions**: Click on links within the article to access quick actions, such as saving the link to Inboxt, opening it in a new tab, or copying the URL.

### Reading settings

You can customize the reader view to match your preferences.

Available options include:

- Theme (light, dark, sepia)
- Font family and size
- Font weight
- Content width
- Line height
- Letter spacing
- Text alignment

Settings apply across all items.

## Highlights

Highlights are saved selections of text from the reader view.

To create a highlight, select text on desktop or mobile.

Highlights:

- Are not comments or annotations
- Sync across devices
- Work the same way for articles and newsletters

## Managing highlights

Inboxt provides a dedicated **Highlights** view where you can:

- See highlights from all saved items in one place
- Open highlights in context
- Copy or delete highlights

## Exporting highlights

Highlights can be exported for use outside of Inboxt.

Supported formats and export behavior are documented in [Exporting Data](/exporting).
